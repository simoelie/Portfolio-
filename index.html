01 / Business problemProblème d’affaires

Business problemProblème d’affaires

How can we identify points of sale at risk of insufficient cash flow early enough to enable faster replenishment and maintain a high level of availability across the territory?Comment identifier suffisamment tôt les points de vente présentant un risque d’insuffisance de cash flow afin d’accélérer le réapprovisionnement et de maintenir un niveau élevé de disponibilité sur le territoire ?

Business problemProblème d’affaires

Availability was around 50%. Insufficient cash flow could limit the ability of points of sale to maintain their operations.La disponibilité était d’environ 50 %. Un cash flow insuffisant pouvait limiter la continuité des opérations des points de vente.

Data insightConstat issu des données

Revenue Tracking highlighted recurring critical situations. This led me to investigate the operational replenishment process.Le Revenue Tracking révélait des situations critiques récurrentes. Ce constat m’a conduit à examiner le processus opérationnel de réapprovisionnement.

Key bottleneckGoulot principal

The distributor largely depended on the point of sale reporting the need. Late visibility and reactive communication delayed the operational trigger.Le distributeur dépendait largement du signalement du besoin par le point de vente. La visibilité tardive et la communication réactive retardaient le déclenchement de l’intervention.

05 / Pain pointsIrritants

Pain pointsIrritants

Reactive replenishmentRéapprovisionnement réactif

Intervention mainly started after the point of sale noticed the need.L’intervention démarrait principalement après le constat du besoin par le point de vente.

Delayed visibilityVisibilité tardive

The distributor was not necessarily informed before the balance became critical.Le distributeur n’était pas nécessairement informé avant que le solde devienne critique.

Manual dependencyDépendance au contact manuel

The point of sale had to initiate contact.Le point de vente devait initier le contact.

Difficult prioritizationPriorisation difficile

Without an early criticality signal, interventions were harder to prioritize.Sans signal de criticité anticipé, les interventions étaient plus difficiles à prioriser.

Availability riskRisque d’indisponibilité

The sequence from falling balance to reporting and replenishment increased availability risk.La succession baisse du solde, signalement et réapprovisionnement augmentait le risque d’indisponibilité.

10 / Outcome / lessonsRésultat / enseignements

Outcome / lessonsRésultat / enseignements

The original account describes an ahead-of-shift reminder followed by a return toward normal. It does not establish a controlled causal effect or a precise improvement figure. Lesson: combine timely operational action with agreed definitions and follow-up measurement.Le récit initial décrit un rappel avant le quart, suivi d’un retour vers la normale. Il n’établit ni effet causal contrôlé ni amélioration chiffrée précise. Enseignement : associer une action opérationnelle à temps à des définitions convenues et à un suivi.

03 / BUSINESS ANALYST PLAYBOOK

How I approach business problems

A practical view of how I clarify needs, structure analysis and help move a business problem toward an actionable solution.

01How do I turn a vague business need into clear requirements?

I start by clarifying the problem, expected outcome, stakeholders and constraints. I then separate needs from proposed solutions, document requirements in clear language and validate them with the people affected before delivery.

Stakeholder analysis · Requirements · User stories · Acceptance criteria

See it in practice →Voir la mise en pratique →

02How do I analyze an inefficient process?

I map the current AS-IS process, identify bottlenecks, handoffs and recurring pain points, then investigate root causes. The TO-BE process is designed around the business objective and validated with stakeholders before implementation.

AS-IS / TO-BE · Process mapping · Root cause analysis · Lean Six Sigma

See it in practice →Voir la mise en pratique →

03How do I handle conflicting stakeholder needs?

I make the competing needs explicit, connect each one to the underlying business objective and assess constraints, impact and dependencies. The goal is to create shared criteria for prioritization rather than treating every request as equally urgent.

Workshops · Stakeholder analysis · Prioritization · Traceability

See it in practice →Voir la mise en pratique →

04How do I choose useful KPIs?

I begin with the decision the team needs to make. A KPI should have a clear definition, reliable data source, relevant comparison point and an action that can follow when the measure changes.

KPIs · Data validation · Power BI · Decision support

See it in practice →Voir la mise en pratique →

05How do I validate that a solution meets the business need?

I link the solution back to the approved requirements and define observable acceptance criteria. During validation or UAT, I focus on real business scenarios, exceptions and whether users can complete the intended outcome.

UAT · Acceptance criteria · Requirements traceability · Feedback

See it in practice →Voir la mise en pratique →

06What do I do when data and stakeholder perceptions disagree?

I verify definitions, data quality, time periods and scope before drawing a conclusion. I use the discrepancy as a question to investigate, combining quantitative evidence with operational context rather than assuming either source is automatically correct.

Data quality · SQL / Excel · Trend analysis · Stakeholder interviews
